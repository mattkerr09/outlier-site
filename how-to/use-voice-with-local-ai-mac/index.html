How to talk to a local AI on your Mac

Outlier · solo-built in Grand Rapids · published 2026-08-14 Last updated 2026-08-14
Quick answer
  • Voice input means dictating instead of typing; voice output means the answer is read aloud.
  • Both happen on your machine, so your voice is not uploaded to a transcription service.
  • It is genuinely faster than typing for long, messy thoughts — which is most thinking.
  • Code-heavy answers are the exception, and the app says so instead of reading symbols aloud.

Dictation gets dismissed as a convenience feature, which undersells it. Talking is how most people produce a first draft fastest — and the reason to do it locally is that your voice is unusually personal data to hand to a server.

Why local voice is different

Cloud dictation ships audio of you speaking to someone else's computer. Even where that is handled carefully, it is a recording of your voice, often capturing whatever else was audible in the room.

Doing it on-device removes the question. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no retention policy to read and no incident that can expose it.

Using it

  1. Start dictating from the composer and speak normally — you don't need to slow down for it.
  2. Send it like any other message; edit first if you want.
  3. Have answers read aloud when you'd rather listen than read.

Where it fits and where it doesn't

  • Good: long rambling context, first drafts, thinking out loud, hands busy.
  • Good: listening to a long answer while doing something else.
  • Poor: code. Reading punctuation and symbols aloud helps nobody — when an answer is all code the app tells you why it isn't reading it, rather than staying silent and looking broken.

Common questions

Can I use voice with an offline AI?

Yes. Dictation and read-aloud both run on your Mac, so they work with no internet and without sending audio anywhere.

Is my voice recording uploaded?

No. Audio is processed on the machine — there is no transcription service in the middle.

Why won't it read a code answer aloud?

Because spoken punctuation and symbols are useless. When an answer is entirely code the app explains that instead of silently doing nothing.

Does voice work with any model tier?

Yes — dictation and read-aloud are independent of which tier you're chatting with.
