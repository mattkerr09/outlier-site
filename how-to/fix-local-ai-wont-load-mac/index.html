Fix: a local AI model won't load on your Mac

Outlier · solo-built in Grand Rapids · published 2026-06-09 Last updated 2026-06-09
Quick answer
  • Most common cause: the model needs more RAM than your Mac has — pick a smaller tier.
  • Second: not enough free disk for the model's weights.
  • Third: an incomplete or corrupted download — re-download the tier.
  • For Vision/Plus, a Metal GPU memory limit can block load on tight-RAM Macs.

When a local AI model won't load on a Mac, it's almost always one of three things: the model needs more RAM than your Mac has, you're low on disk, or the download didn't finish. Here's how to tell which one it is and fix it.

Cause 1: the model is too big for your RAM

This is the usual one. Each tier has a memory floor, and Apple Silicon shares RAM between CPU and GPU. If you ask for a model your Mac can't hold, it refuses to load rather than crash.

ModelNeeds
Nano 4B / Lite 9B16 GB
Core / Code / Vision (27B–35B)24–32 GB
Plus 397B64 GB

Fix: switch to a tier your Mac supports. On 16 GB, stay on Nano/Lite. On 32 GB, Core 27B is the ceiling for comfortable use.

Cause 2: not enough disk space

A model can't load if its weights didn't fully download, and they won't download if the disk is full. Core-class models need ~15 GB; Plus needs ~209 GB. Fix: free up space (see freeing disk space for AI models), then retry the download.

Cause 3: incomplete or corrupted download

If a download was interrupted, the model may be partial and fail to load. Fix: remove the tier in the app's model manager and re-download it on a stable connection. Your chats and settings are unaffected.

Cause 4: Metal GPU memory limit (Vision / Plus)

On Macs near the RAM floor for the big multimodal or 397B models, macOS's Metal GPU memory limit can block a load even when disk is fine. Fix: close other heavy apps to free memory, restart the app, and if it persists, use a smaller tier. The largest models simply want a roomier Mac.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app say a model needs more memory?

Because that tier's weights don't fit in your Mac's unified RAM alongside macOS. The app refuses to load it rather than crash. Switch to a smaller tier, or use a Mac with more RAM.

I have the RAM but it still won't load — why?

Check disk space and the download. A partial or interrupted download fails to load; remove the tier and re-download it. For the largest models, closing other heavy apps and restarting can clear a Metal GPU memory limit.

Will switching tiers lose my chats?

No. Switching or removing a model only affects the weights on disk. Your conversations, projects, and settings stay intact.
