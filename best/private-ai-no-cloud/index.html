What 'private AI' really means

Plenty of products slap "private" on the box and then ship your prompts off to a server anyway. Real privacy is simpler than that. The model runs locally and your data never leaves the device. Anything else is a weaker promise. Encryption in transit, a no-training pledge: nice, but you're still trusting a vendor to hold your data and behave.

So the line is this simple. Cloud AI sends your data out. Local AI doesn't.

The one test that matters

Turn off your wifi and use the tool. Still answering? Then inference is happening on your machine and nothing's going out. The moment it stalls, you know your data was headed for someone's server. Every tool below passes this once the models are downloaded.

What to verify yourself

  • Offline: pull the network and see if it keeps working. Don't take anyone's word for it.
  • Telemetry: does the app phone home while you use it? Outlier runs none on inference.
  • Open weights: can you actually inspect the model? Outlier publishes its on HuggingFace.
  • Where data is stored: on your local disk, where you control it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most private AI tool?

Any AI that runs entirely on your device, because your data never leaves it. In 2026 that means local tools like Outlier, Ollama, LM Studio, Jan, and llama.cpp. The test is simple: if it works with the internet off, it's private.

Is ChatGPT private?

No — ChatGPT sends your prompts to OpenAI's servers to process them. Even with chat history or training turned off, the data still leaves your device. For true privacy you need a model that runs locally.

How do I verify an AI is really private?

Turn off your internet and use it. If it keeps working, the model is running on your machine and nothing is being sent out. Also check for no inference telemetry and open, auditable model weights.
