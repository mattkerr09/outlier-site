Can a Mac run gpt-oss 20B?

Quick answer

Yes. gpt-oss 20B needs about 24 GB even for short chats and the same 24 GB for long 32,000-token work, by our cautious rule, not measured by us.

On the MacBook Pro M1 Pro, expect about 21 to 42 tokens/s, estimated. On the Mac Studio M1 Ultra, expect about 83 to 167 tokens/s, estimated.

This download lets you chat and help with code. It takes 11.18 GB of disk space. At 21B MoE / 3.6B active, it sits alongside 15 smaller models and 17 larger models in our comparison by total parameter count. MoE means mixture of experts, with the active part used for each token.

This is the smaller gpt-oss download in our list, with support for chat and coding. It uses a mixture of experts, so the active part is smaller than the whole model. The exact model ID is gpt-oss-20b.

How we work this out

Weights take 11.18 GB. L = 24 attention layers, kv_heads = 8, head_dim = 64. We count every attention layer in full and keep all expert weights in memory.

KV GB = 2 × L × kv_heads × head_dim × 2 bytes × tokens ÷ 1,000,000,000. The cache is 16-bit.

Need = weights + rounded KV + runtime, rounded to two decimals. Runtime and activations take 2 GB. GPU limits below are rounded to eight decimals.

On a Mac, macOS lets the GPU use about two thirds of memory by default. A 16 GB Mac has about 10.7 GB for the model, less than the 11.18 GB of 4-bit weights this page's file takes. OpenAI's 16 GB figure is for its own MXFP4 weights on a GPU. Our 24 GB short-chat figure comes from our cautious rule, with a 16-bit cache and every attention layer counted in full.
