What AI models can a Mac with 8 GB run?

Outlier · Figures as of 8 November 2026

Quick answer
  • A Mac with 8 GB fits 5 models for short chats, 4 for long 32,000-token work, from 33 models in our list. We use measured minimums where available and our cautious rule for the rest.
  • For long work, the roomiest fit is Qwen3.5-4B. It has the lowest listed memory floor, 6 GB, leaving 2 GB above that floor.
  • For long work, the smallest fit by parameter count is Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, listed as 0.5B dense. It takes 0.28 GB on disk. The largest fit by parameter count is Qwen3.5-4B, listed as 4B dense.
