Can a Mac run Qwen3.8 27B?

Quick answer

Yes. Qwen3.8 27B has a measured minimum of 24 GB, from build 1.11.792 on 2026-08-19.

We measured 17.2 tokens/s on our Mac Studio M1 Ultra. On the MacBook Pro M1 Pro, expect about 3.4 to 8.4 tokens/s, estimated.
