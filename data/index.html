Original datasets — head-to-head outputs against Claude Opus, paged inference engine measurements, and side-by-side comparison with the other Mac-native local-AI tools. Every quantitative claim on outlier.host traces back to one of these pages or to the FINAL_LAUNCH_NUMBERS source file. Numbers we haven't validated to our own bar are explicitly marked.

The datasets

Outlier vs Claude Opus 4.7 — 54-prompt head-to-head

Same prompts, two models. Six tests captured verbatim from both Claude and Outlier on disk. A 54-prompt rubric bench scored Outlier 99.5% on flagship-quality features. Reproducible.

V11 streaming engine benchmarks (and why V10 ships instead)

K-sweep results across Plus 397B and Vision 35B on Mac Studio M1 Ultra. Five-run separate-process methodology. Includes the honest finding that pushed us to remove V11 in favor of V10.

Local AI on Mac in 2026 — Outlier vs Ollama vs LM Studio vs Jan

Side-by-side comparison across seven dimensions, including the dimension where Outlier is presently the only option (paged-MoE inference for models bigger than RAM).

Why publish raw data

Most product sites publish benchmark headlines. Outlier publishes the prompts, the outputs, the methodology, the stderr, the dates, and the source files. The point is not to be impressive — it's to be checkable. If you can't reproduce a number on your own Mac in the same conditions, the number doesn't belong on this site.

If you find one we got wrong, that's a real bug. Issue on GitHub.
