Do AI chatbots send your chats to trackers? Two 2026 studies, explained

Matthew Kerr · Outlier · published 2026-11-20 Last updated 2026-11-20
Quick answer

Some AI chat websites sent conversation information to trackers under the studies' test conditions. Your chats are never on our servers, because we never receive them. Outlier's local chat runs on your Mac, with no chat web page or trackers in the chat; the dated lsof capture below shows the engine check.

Two studies asked related questions

A tracker is an outside service used by a website to record activity. A conversation title can reveal a topic even when the full message is absent. These studies looked at information reaching trackers through AI chat websites. They used different methods and reported different results.

How to read the difference

The Stony Brook counts describe observed leakage under the study's test conditions. The IMDEA report describes tracker use and potential transmission of conversation information. Finding a tracker and observing a verbatim prompt reach it are different findings. The numbers from one study should not be attached to the other.

Neither result makes a lasting promise about any named service. A result under the study's test conditions covers that test. Different methods and results do not support calling a service clean or declaring every cloud chat unsafe. For a particular app, check the date, the feature in use and what information was actually observed leaving.

Settings you can review now

  • Use your browser's tracker blocking options. They may reduce requests to outside trackers. They do not change the fact that a hosted model receives the prompt you send it.
  • Consider signed-out use where the service offers it. This can avoid attaching a signed-in account to the session, but it does not establish that tracking stops.
  • Read the app's own privacy settings. Check controls for history, training and connected features separately. A training setting alone does not answer which web requests a page makes.

These are choices to review, not guarantees that a browser session has no sharing. For work that should stay off a chat website, a local model offers another way to do the task.

A local chat and a marketing website are separate

A local app with no chat web page and no trackers in its chat gives a website tracker no chat page to read.
