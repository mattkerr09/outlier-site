Outlier pricing

Last updated 2026-11-20

Start with Free for everyday chat on your Mac. Choose Pro for the other four model tiers and every feature. Both plans run local AI on your own Mac. There is no monthly plan and no token bill.

Free: Nano and Lite

Free costs $0 and stays free. It is for everyday private chat, with no account needed. It includes:

  • Nano 4B and Lite 9B.
  • Local chat and saved sessions.
  • The model picker and model downloads.
  • An 8K context window per conversation.

Nano needs 6 GB of memory and Lite needs 12 GB. Both fit a 16 GB MacBook Air. Start here to see how local chat works before you pay.

Pro: the Outlier Lifetime Licence

Pro adds Quick, Core, Vision and Plus to Nano and Lite. You get all six tiers and every feature, including the coding agent, local memory, projects, a 128K context window and on-device fine-tuning. Quick needs 16 GB to load and wants 24 GB. Core and Vision need 24 GB.

The licence activates on up to 3 Macs at a time. Pay once to keep everything Pro has, including future model releases, for as long as Outlier exists. There is no subscription, no renewal and no per-token charge for local generation.

Pricing FAQ

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is one payment for the Outlier Lifetime Licence. There is no subscription or renewal. Local generation is never metered or charged per token.

What happens after December 2?

The Black Friday sale ends. From December 3, Outlier is $39 once and the bundle is $79 once. Nano and Lite stay free.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Free includes Nano and Lite, local chat and sessions, the model picker and downloads, and an 8K context window per conversation. No account is needed.
