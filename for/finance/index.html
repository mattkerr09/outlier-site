Finance and accounting run on confidential data — client books, deal models, material non-public information. Cloud AI tools want all of it on their servers. Local AI keeps it on your Mac: Outlier runs models on-device, so sensitive financial data is never transmitted to a third-party AI vendor.

Why confidential financial data shouldn't go to cloud AI

Client financial records, deal models, audit workpapers, and MNPI are exactly the kind of data that shouldn't be pasted into a consumer chatbot. Beyond confidentiality agreements, firms in regulated contexts have recordkeeping and information-barrier obligations that a cloud round-trip complicates.

The safe default many teams land on is "no AI for anything client-sensitive" — which means giving up real productivity on analysis and drafting.

How local AI keeps the data in your control

Run the model on your own Mac and the data never leaves. Outlier processes everything locally, stores conversations on your disk, and works with the network off. There's no third-party server holding your spreadsheets and no vendor terms governing what happens to them.

What it does for finance work

  • Explaining and sanity-checking spreadsheet logic and formulas
  • Drafting memos, commentary, and client-ready narrative
  • Summarizing filings, contracts, and long reports
  • Reconciliation and data-cleanup reasoning
  • Reviewing scanned statements with the Vision model

Always verify the numbers — language models can miscalculate. Use it to accelerate analysis you check, not to produce figures you trust blind.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use AI on client financial data?

With local AI, yes — because the data stays on your Mac and is never sent to a third-party vendor. Confirm it fits your firm's confidentiality and recordkeeping policies. Verify any numbers the model produces.

Does it handle spreadsheets?

It reasons about spreadsheet logic, explains formulas, and drafts commentary from data you paste in. It is not a calculator — always check computed figures against your source.

Is this investment advice?

No. Outlier is a general-purpose AI app, not a source of financial or investment advice. Outputs require professional review.
