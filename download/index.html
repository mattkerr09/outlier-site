Download Outlier for Mac

Outlier is a desktop app for Apple Silicon Macs that runs AI models on your own machine. The download is one signed, notarized app of about 290 MB. The models are separate: the app fetches each one when you first choose its tier, and it stays on your disk.

What you need

  • An Apple Silicon Mac, M1 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • macOS 26 or later. It is a free update.
  • About 290 MB for the app, plus disk space for the models you choose (sizes below).
  • An internet connection to download the app and each model. After that, Outlier keeps answering with Wi-Fi off.

Install it in four steps

  1. Click Download Outlier for Mac above. You get a single .dmg file.
  2. Open it and drag Outlier into Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.
  3. Launch Outlier and choose a tier. Nano and Lite are free and don't ask for an account; the model lands in a folder on your Mac and stays there.
  4. Ask one question while you are still online to confirm the model loaded. From then on the answers come from the file on your disk, so they keep coming with Wi-Fi off.

Questions before you download

Does Outlier run on an Intel Mac?

No. It needs Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 26 or later, which is a free update.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free tiers. Download the app, pick Nano or Lite, and start. Neither plan has a token bill.

How much disk space should I plan for?

About 290 MB for the app, then anywhere from 2.37 GB (Nano) to 209 GB (Plus), depending on the tiers you use. Remove a tier in the app's model manager and the space comes back; your chats and settings stay.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a model is on your disk it runs with the network off. Web search is optional and only runs when you switch it on.
