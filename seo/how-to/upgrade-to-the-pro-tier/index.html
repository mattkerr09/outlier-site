How to upgrade to Outlier Pro

Last updated 2026-08-24 · Outlier v1.11.804

Quick answer

Pro unlocks the Quick, Core, Vision 3.8, and Plus tiers. The whole sequence below stays on the Mac.

What you need first for “how to upgrade to outlier pro”

Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 12 or later, the unified-memory minimum that the chosen tier requires (6 GB for Nano, 12 GB for Lite, 16 GB for Quick, 24 GB for Core and Vision 3.8, 64 GB for Plus). Internet is required only for the one-time model download.

Steps

  1. Open Settings > Pro. The Pro section appears on the General tab in v1.11.469.
  2. Buy through Dodo Payments. Pro is a one-time purchase: Founders Lifetime at $249.
  3. Paste your license key. Settings > license > Activate. The key is verified against whichever issuer sold it — live.dodopayments.com for new purchases, api.polar.sh for keys bought before 23 August 2026 — and your tier is derived from the key.
  4. Restart the app. The Pro-gated tiers appear in the model picker after restart.

What is the specific thing to know about this guide?

Pro tier gating uses a license key: paste it into Settings > license > Activate and the app verifies it against the issuer that sold it — live.dodopayments.com for keys bought now, or api.polar.sh for keys bought before 23 August 2026 — then derives your tier from the key. Checkout issues the key straight after payment.

What can go wrong with this guide?

  • Save your license key in your password manager; you re-enter it in Settings > license > Activate when you reinstall.
  • Refunds are handled by the payment processor — Dodo Payments for new purchases — not by Outlier directly.

Cloud Pro tiers gate behind a server-side license check tied to your account.
