What does Outlier Quick run like on MacBook Air 15 M4?

The pairing under examination is Outlier Quick (15.61 GB MLX 4-bit, Gemma-4-26B-a4b-it base, 32K default context, 16 GB unified memory minimum) on a MacBook Air 15 M4 (Apple M4, 10 CPU cores, 10 GPU cores, 16|24|32 GB unified memory, 120 GB/s, 2025). Outlier loads this tier as one mlx_lm process inside the bundled FastAPI sidecar. Apple Silicon decode on dense 4-bit weights is bandwidth-bound, so the ceiling on this exact MacBook Air 15 M4 scales as the ratio 120 / 800 = 0.15× the published M1 Ultra number.

What context window can I use on a 16 GB Mac?

The Outlier Quick tier defaults to 32K context and caps at 256K. KV cache scales linearly with context length on dense models, so longer contexts trade headroom for capacity. On a 16|24|32 GB MacBook Air 15 M4, the default context is the safe starting point.
