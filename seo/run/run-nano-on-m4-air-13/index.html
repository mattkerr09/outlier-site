Run Outlier Nano on MacBook Air 13 M4

Last updated 2026-06-18 · Outlier v1.11.469

Quick answer

Outlier Nano on MacBook Air 13 M4: fits comfortably. Minimum unified memory required: 6 GB. Disk size: 2.37 GB. Default context window: 32K tokens. Operator-reported throughput: ~32 tok/s on a 16 GB M4 Air (2026-05-04, single-prompt observation, not yet σ-qualified — treat as indicative).

What does Outlier Nano run like on MacBook Air 13 M4?

The pairing under examination is Outlier Nano (2.37 GB MLX 4-bit, Qwen3.5-4B base, 32K default context, 6 GB unified memory minimum) on a MacBook Air 13 M4 (Apple M4, 10 CPU cores, 8-10 GPU cores, 16|24|32 GB unified memory, 120 GB/s, 2025). Outlier loads this tier as one mlx_lm process inside the bundled FastAPI sidecar. Apple Silicon decode on dense 4-bit weights is bandwidth-bound, so the ceiling on this exact MacBook Air 13 M4 scales as the ratio 120 / 800 = 0.15× the published M1 Ultra number.

How much memory does the model take during generation?

Outlier’s Outlier Nano has a measured peak generation footprint of about 3 GB. The MacBook Air 13 M4 base configuration ships with 16|24|32 GB unified, and the rule of thumb is to leave roughly 4 GB for the OS and one open browser tab. Operator-reported throughput: ~32 tok/s on a 16 GB M4 Air (2026-05-04, single-prompt observation, not yet σ-qualified — treat as indicative).

What is the install path on MacBook Air 13 M4 for Outlier Nano?

The 2025 MacBook Air 13 M4 ships with 16|24|32 GB of unified memory, so headroom for the 6 GB Outlier Nano requirement is 10 GB on the base SKU. Step-by-step install instructions live on the install guide; the part that varies for this Apple M4 machine is that the 2.37 GB pull from Outlier-Ai/Outlier-Nano-4B-MLX-4bit over HTTPS lands in ~/Library/Application Support/Outlier/models/nano/ and download time is bandwidth-limited by the network, not by the 10-core Apple M4 CPU.

What context window can I use on a 16 GB Mac?

The Outlier Nano tier defaults to 32K context and caps at 256K. KV cache scales linearly with context length on dense models, so longer contexts trade headroom for capacity. On a 16|24|32 GB MacBook Air 13 M4, the default context is the safe starting point.

What works well on this pairing, and what is still rough?

  • The MacBook Air 13 M4 (Apple M4, 120 GB/s) is bandwidth-bound on dense 4-bit decode; the throughput ceiling for Outlier Nano on this exact machine is approximately 120 divided by 2.37 GB tokens per second.
  • The 16 GB base SKU of the MacBook Air 13 M4 swaps aggressively under the Outlier Nano tier; close Chrome and other RAM-heavy applications before generation.
  • The Apple M4 (120 GB/s, 8-10 GPU cores) is current Apple Store silicon as of 2025; new-purchase tok-per-second-per-dollar is best in the lineup at the Outlier Nano tier.
  • Cold boot of Outlier on a 10-core Apple M4 is roughly 50 seconds while the bundled Python.framework warms; this is documented on the v1.9 backlog under the boot-time investigation thread.

What is the unique number for Outlier Nano on MacBook Air 13 M4?
